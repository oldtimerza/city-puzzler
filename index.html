Factory sites

4 Factories · Standard

More Factories mean more Steel districts, tighter row, column, and district choices, and more supplier links to plan.

Goal

Place every active building icon once in each row, column, and district so every district receives its required resources. Tutorial levels introduce the building types one rule at a time.

Rules

Each building icon must appear once in every row, column, and district unless a level says otherwise. A cell may contain only one building. Dots in a district's upper corner show resources it still needs: green for food, blue for water, gold for power, and red for steel.

Solar panel

Cannot touch a dam orthogonally.

Dam

Cannot touch a solar panel. It irrigates nearby farms.

Farm

Must touch a dam orthogonally before the plan is complete.

Factory

Makes steel when it touches both a Solar Panel and a Dam.

How to play

Select a building, then click a highlighted legal cell. Click a placed building to remove it. Use T for one highlighted hint, and Undo or Redo to revise a plan.
