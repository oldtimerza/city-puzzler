Goal

Place every active symbol once in each row, column, and district. Floating requirement markers point to districts that still need symbols. Tutorial levels introduce one relationship at a time.

Rules

Each symbol must appear once in every row, column, and region unless a level says otherwise. A cell may contain only one symbol. A callout arrow identifies each region's missing symbols.

A red slash over a Triangle or Square means it does not currently meet its support requirements. It does not count toward its region's needs until the slash disappears.

District tunnels

Two separate parts with the same district are joined by an arched tunnel line. They count as one district for quotas and requirements, but the arch is not an edge: shapes on opposite ends do not support, conflict with, or touch one another.

Circle

Cannot share an edge with Diamond.

Diamond

Cannot share an edge with Circle.

Triangle

Counts when it shares an edge with Diamond.

Square

Counts when it shares an edge with both Circle and Diamond.

How to play

Click an empty cell to choose from the symbols that can legally go there. Use Show placements to display every currently legal symbol, Resolve singles to fill the first single-option cell, or Requirements to switch between all callouts and hover-only callouts. Click a placed symbol to remove it. Use T for one highlighted hint, and Undo or Redo to revise a plan.
